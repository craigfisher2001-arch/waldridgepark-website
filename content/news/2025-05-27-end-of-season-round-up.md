What a season it has been for Waldridge Park JFC. Across all age groups, from our youngest Superstars right through to the adult teams, we've seen some fantastic football, brilliant teamwork, and — most importantly — plenty of smiles.

## Junior teams

Our younger age groups have had a wonderful introduction to football this season. The Superstars sessions have been packed every week, and it's been brilliant to see so many new faces joining the club.

Further up the age groups, several of our teams took part in regional festivals and tournaments, giving players the chance to represent the club and experience football in a competitive but fun environment.

## Adult teams

The Saturday 1st and Reserves have both had solid campaigns. The Lumley women's team have also had a strong season and continue to grow — if you know anyone who'd like to get involved, please point them our way.

## Looking ahead

Summer training details will be confirmed shortly by individual coaches. Keep an eye on this page and our Facebook page for updates.

Thank you to every player, parent, coach and volunteer who has made this season what it was. See you in August!
