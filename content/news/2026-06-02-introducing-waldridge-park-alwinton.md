Getting ready to begin their grassroots football journey this month is our newest team: Waldridge Park Alwinton. Having been with our Superstars group for many months, this group of players has reached the age where they are ready to move into competitive football. 

Led by Carrie, Alwinton will be playing in the North East Junior Football league where they will face different clubs from around the region. As anyone who has been involved in under 7s football will tell you, this is such an exciting team for the players as they get their first taste of 'proper' football. Their first wins, their first defeats, their first goals, it is here where dreams begin. It may look like semi-organised chaos at times but the players are learning every time they step on the pitch.

Please join me in wishing Carrie and the team the very best of luck!
