## Presentation Events 2026

We look forward to welcome all of our players, their coaches, and their families to Witton Gilbert Working Mens Club a week on Saturday to celebrate another fantastic season of grassroots football.

Starting at 1pm on Saturday 13th June and continuing throughout the weekend, players will receive their participation trophies, as well as finding out who from their team has won Player's Player of the Year and Manager's Player of the Year. We will celebrate those who have served the club for six and ten years as well as other individuals who have made exceptional contributions to the club this year.

![The Gordon Catterell Trophy](/images/uploads/view-recent-photos.png "The Gordon Catterell Trophy")

Dont forget to bring some spare change with you. As always we will have snacks and drinks to purchase, as well as the ever-popular bottle tombola and other games to have a go at. The bar will also be open for drinks throughout the weekend.

Speak to your coach to find out which session your team is attending. We will see you there!
