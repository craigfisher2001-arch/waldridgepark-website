We are looking forward to welcoming players, coaches and families along to Witton Gilbert Working Mens Club for the annual Waldridge Park Presentation events. Come along and celebrate another fantastic season of grassroots football with all of our teams.

Dont forget to bring some spare change along with you as there will be food and drinks available to purchase along with the ever popular bottle tombola and raffle.

Sessions begin on Saturday 13th June at 1pm and continue throughout the weekend. Speak to your coach to find out which is the right session for your team.

See you all there!
