Who we are

A club built by the community, for the community

Waldridge Park JFC was founded in 1988 by a group of parents in Barford Drive, Chester-le-Street. What started as children kicking a ball on the estate grass has grown into one of the area's most loved junior clubs — with teams spanning from our Superstars U5 sessions right through to adult football.

We believe in measuring success in smiles. Every player, regardless of ability or background, gets the same brilliant support and treatment.
