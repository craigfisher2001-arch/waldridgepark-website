About Waldridge Park JFC

A community club built on passion, belonging, and the belief that football is for everyone.

Our story

Founded in a cul-de-sac, built for a community

Waldridge Park JFC was founded in 1988 by three parents — Alan Harman, Malcolm Cole, and Gordon Catterell — all living on Barford Drive in Chester-le-Street. Their boys would play on the grass in the cul-de-sac until some neighbours complained. So Alan, Malcolm and Gordon suggested they bring friends over to the Hermitage field one evening.

Several children from the estate turned up and a club was born. They attended a Russell Foster meeting in June and — as Alan puts it — "foolishly said we were a new team although we had nothing." By August they needed a pitch, strips, nets, and funds.

"I contacted my friend Peter Beardsley and asked if he could help. He donated a signed Liverpool shirt which was sold for £400 — and that gave us a start." That £400 has since grown into one of County Durham's most loved junior clubs, with over 20 teams and players from U5 right through to adult football.

"Full circle — my grandson plays for the club and lives in our old close." — Alan Harman, founder

How we play

Our philosophy

Everything we do at Waldridge Park flows from four principles. They guide our coaching, our culture, and the way we treat every player.

Play

Enjoy the ball, want the ball, share the ball. Let the players play. Football at its heart is a game of joy — we never forget that.

Build

Build from the back, build skills, build players, build teams, build a community club. Development over results, always.

Be game based

Training activities need to reflect games and have a high percentage of ball rolling time. No standing in lines — always learning through playing.

Attitude & application

Try hard, be brave on the ball, recover well and enjoy it. We measure success in the smiles on players' faces, not on the scoreboard.
