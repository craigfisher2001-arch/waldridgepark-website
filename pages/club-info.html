Membership

Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are £20 per calendar month, with a 10% discount for multiple siblings.

Standing orders should be made payable to Waldridge Park Football Club. Please contact the club directly for bank details — we don't publish these online.

For queries about subscriptions, please email waldridgepark@yahoo.co.uk
